## Privacy review

Before publication, the five images were reviewed for URLs/IP addresses, API keys, credentials, cookies/tokens, account/order/trade/position identifiers, balances/equity, raw payloads, and infrastructure or deployment details. None were present. The captures retain only permissible aggregated historical results, trade counts, fees, Bybit/Pionex labels, and trading-pair names.

## Interpretation

The screens show post-trade historical analytics. They do not establish causality, provide a trading recommendation, or predict future performance. Sample-size labels are visibility cues, not claims of statistical significance.
